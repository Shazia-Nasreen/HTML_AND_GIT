Flags of the World

Sverige

The flag of Sweden features a blue field with a gold Scandinavian cross that extends to the edges of the flag. It represents Sweden's deep-rooted culture and history.

Frankrike

France's flag, known as the Tricolore, consists of three vertical bands of blue, white, and red. It symbolizes liberty, equality, and fraternity, values deeply ingrained in French culture.

Tyskland

The German flag features three horizontal stripes in black, red, and yellow, representing the unity and freedom of the German people throughout history.

Pakistan

Pakistan's flag features a dark green field with a white vertical stripe, a crescent moon, and a five-pointed star. These symbols represent Islam and reflect the country’s heritage.
